My TripleTen Journey

Every single project I have ever conducted during my TripleTen journey is displayed in this humble portfolio. 

This portfolio showcases three data analysis projects from my TripleTen journey, demonstrating skills in business analytics, spreadsheet analysis, and data visualization.

Projects (As they appear in my folder):

E-commerce Conversion Funnel & Cohort Analysis

Analyzed user activity data to evaluate website performance and identify gaps in converting product views into purchases. Utilized advanced spreadsheets, pivot tables, and functions such as VLOOKUP, TEXT, and DATEDIF to build a conversion funnel and cohort-based retention analysis. Identified drop-off points in the conversion funnel and varying retention rates across customer cohorts over time. Provided insights to improve user conversion and retention by optimizing key stages of the customer journey.

Manhattan Airbnb Market Analysis

Analyzed Airbnb listing data to identify the most attractive neighborhoods and property types for vacation rental investment. Utilized advanced spreadsheets, pivot tables, and functions such as SUMIF to evaluate rental activity, segment listings, and estimate annual revenue. Identified top-performing neighborhoods and found that 1-bedroom units (and studios in Midtown) generated the highest engagement and revenue potential. Provided data-driven recommendations to prioritize high-demand property types and locations to maximize rental income.

Superstore Tableau Worksheet Analysis

Analyzed Superstore sales data to identify key drivers of profit and loss and evaluate advertising opportunities. Used Tableau to create interactive dashboards with calculated fields and joins, analyzing trends across products, regions, and time periods. Identified top profit-generating segments, underperforming subcategories, and optimal state-month combinations for targeted advertising. Recommended focusing on high-margin products, eliminating consistent loss-makers, and strategically investing up to 20% of profits in advertising during peak performance periods
